The v2 port follows the `port-freelens-extension-to-v2` skill of
freelensapp/freelens-example-extension: one migration issue, a copy of
freelensapp/freelens-example-extension#326, and one issue per feature. The
specs hold the design and the tests; the issues hold the steps and their
checklists.
